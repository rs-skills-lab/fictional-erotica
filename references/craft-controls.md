# Erotic Scene Craft Controls / 情色场景写作控制

Use these controls for long, explicit, structurally complex, or revision-heavy scenes. Apply them internally and selectively; do not turn the story into a checklist.

这些控制项用于长篇、直白、结构复杂或需要大量修订的场景。在内部有选择地运用，不要把故事变成核对表。

## Heat Contract / 热度契约

Choose one starting contract and change it only when the user asks:

选择一个起始契约，只有用户要求时才改变：

| Level / 等级 | On-page contract / 页面呈现契约 |
|---|---|
| Closed / 闭门 | Desire and emotional consequence; sex occurs off-page / 呈现欲望与情感后果；性行为发生在页面之外 |
| Sensual / 感官 | Touch and arousal are present; mechanics stay limited / 呈现触碰与唤起；身体机制保持有限 |
| Open-door / 开放式 | Acts and bodies are clear; sensation and emotion share focus / 行为与身体清楚；感觉与情感共同成为焦点 |
| Explicit / 明确 | Concrete anatomy and actions are sustained where permitted / 在允许范围内持续呈现具体器官与动作 |
| Sex-primary / 性主导 | Sexual experience is the scene's main narrative subject / 性经验是场景的主要叙事对象 |

Heat is not the same as intensity. A quiet scene can be explicit; a fully clothed scene can be intensely erotic.

热度不等于强度。安静的场景可以非常直白；衣着完整的场景也可以具有极强情色张力。

## Scene State Vector / 场景状态向量

Track these variables privately while drafting:

起草时在内部追踪这些变量：

- fictional form, reality boundary, and any source material deliberately transformed / 虚构形式、现实边界，以及任何经过有意改写的源材料；
- relationship stage and current emotional distance / 关系阶段与当前情感距离；
- each person's immediate want and unspoken want / 每个人的即时欲望与未说出口的欲望；
- consent signals, boundaries, and any condition requiring a check-in / 同意信号、边界，以及任何需要再次确认的状态；
- body positions, occupied hands, clothing state, objects, and available space / 身体位置、双手状态、衣着、物件与可用空间；
- arousal, comfort, fatigue, pain, and overstimulation / 唤起、舒适度、疲惫、疼痛与过度刺激；
- pace, power, speech register, and who initiates the next change / 速度、权力、语言调性，以及谁发起下一次变化；
- what has changed since the previous beat / 与前一节拍相比发生了什么变化。

If two consecutive paragraphs leave every variable unchanged, combine or cut them.

如果连续两段没有改变任何变量，合并或删减它们。

## Response Loop / 回应循环

Build embodied causality:

建立具身因果关系：

1. One person acts / 一个人行动。
2. The other produces a specific signal / 另一个人给出具体信号。
3. The viewpoint character reads that signal / 视角人物解读该信号。
4. They continue, alter, stop, tease, ask, or yield in response / 他们据此继续、调整、停止、逗弄、询问、顺从或让出主动权。

Do not make the loop perfectly efficient. Established lovers may use shorthand; new lovers may misread and correct; playful partners may deliberately delay an answer.

不要让循环完美高效。稳定恋人可能使用速记；新恋人可能误读后纠正；喜欢游戏的伴侣可能故意延迟回应。

## Risk-Sensitive Craft / 风险敏感写作

Treat breath restriction, restraint, pain, substances, weapon-themed roleplay, impaired capacity, and other risk-sensitive elements through one general craft rule: establish the adult fictional frame, keep choice and changed capacity legible, write non-instructionally, track material consequences and aftermath, and never turn actual danger or inability to signal into proof of love, trust, or pleasure. Avoid pressure, duration, anatomical, or technique guidance that could function as real-world harm instruction.

用一条上位写作原则处理呼吸限制、束缚、疼痛、物质影响、武器主题角色扮演、行动能力受损及其他风险敏感元素：确立成年虚构框架，使人物的选择与状态变化清楚可辨，以非指导性方式书写，追踪物质后果与事后状态，并且绝不把真实危险或无法发出信号写成爱、信任或快感的证明。避免提供可能构成现实伤害指导的力度、时长、解剖或技术细节。

## Dialogue Matrix / 对白矩阵

Read erotic dialogue on three layers:

从三个层面阅读情色对白：

- **Text / 文本**: the literal request, observation, joke, praise, refusal, confession, or command / 字面上的请求、观察、玩笑、赞美、拒绝、坦白或命令。
- **Subtext / 潜台词**: what the speaker wants but cannot or will not say directly / 说话者想要却不能或不愿直接说出的内容。
- **Context / 语境**: history, power, trust, embarrassment, familiarity, and what this line means from this person now / 历史、权力、信任、尴尬、熟悉程度，以及这句话此刻由这个人说出意味着什么。

Give each speaker a voice fingerprint:

赋予每位说话者语言指纹：

- vocabulary and metaphor range / 词汇与比喻范围；
- sentence length and grammatical completeness / 句子长度与语法完整程度；
- directness, humor, formality, and profanity / 直接程度、幽默、正式程度与脏话；
- preferred questions, commands, praise, or evasions / 偏好的提问、命令、赞美或回避方式；
- words, pet names, or admissions they would never use / 绝不会使用的词、昵称或坦白；
- how speech changes under pleasure, fear, anger, tenderness, or fatigue / 语言在快感、恐惧、愤怒、温柔或疲惫中如何变化。

Apply the double-duty test: keep lines that perform at least two useful functions, such as guiding action while exposing history, or teasing while checking consent.

执行双重功能测试：保留至少完成两项有效功能的台词，例如在引导行动的同时暴露历史，或在逗弄的同时确认同意。

## Sensory Selection / 感官选择

Choose two or three dominant channels per beat:

每个节拍选择两到三个主导通道：

- pressure, stretch, friction, balance, weight, muscular effort / 压力、拉伸、摩擦、平衡、重量、肌肉用力；
- temperature, breath, pulse, dampness, texture / 温度、呼吸、脉搏、湿润、质地；
- sound, silence, broken speech, laughter / 声音、沉默、破碎语言、笑声；
- smell or taste when the viewpoint would plausibly attend to it / 当视角人物确实可能注意时使用气味或味道；
- sight when eye contact, posture, expression, or exposure changes the scene / 当目光接触、姿态、表情或暴露改变场景时使用视觉。

Avoid a five-sense inventory. Link each sensory detail to a consequence in action, thought, language, or desire.

避免罗列五感。让每个感官细节都在行动、思想、语言或欲望中产生后果。

## Long-Form Continuity Ledger / 长篇连续性账本

For repeated scenes across a story, remember:

跨越故事的反复场景中，记住：

- established preferences and hard boundaries / 已确立的偏好与硬边界；
- acts or language already tried, liked, disliked, or negotiated / 已经尝试、喜欢、不喜欢或协商过的行为与语言；
- injuries, medication, contraception, accessibility, and recovery needs / 伤病、药物、避孕、无障碍与恢复需求；
- recurring jokes, private vocabulary, and evolving dirty talk / 反复出现的玩笑、私人词汇与不断发展的情色语言；
- what each intimate scene revealed or changed in the relationship / 每个亲密场景揭示或改变了关系中的什么。

Escalation should come from new meaning, vulnerability, play, or circumstance, not merely from making every later scene mechanically more extreme.

递进应来自新的意义、脆弱、游戏或情境，而不是机械地让每个后续场景都更加极端。

## Five-Pass Audit / 五轮审计

Run in this order:

按以下顺序执行：

1. **Agency / 能动性**: mutuality and boundaries are legible; intensity does not erase consent / 相互性与边界清楚可辨；强度不能抹去人物的选择权。
2. **Character / 人物**: cover the names and check whether the speakers still sound distinguishable / 遮住姓名，检查说话者是否仍能彼此区分。
3. **Continuity / 连续性**: positions, hands, clothes, objects, and transitions remain physically possible / 位置、双手、衣物、物件与转换在物理上保持可能。
4. **Escalation / 递进**: each beat changes state; repetition earns emphasis rather than filling space / 每个节拍都改变状态；重复必须强化意义，而不是填充篇幅。
5. **Prose / 行文**: remove filter words, generic reactions, repeated synonyms, stacked metaphors, and explanation after showing / 删除过滤词、通用反应、同义反复、堆叠比喻，以及已经呈现之后的解释。
