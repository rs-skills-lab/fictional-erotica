## Fictional Boundary / 虚构边界

Infer only enough fictional structure to make desire, action, and consequence coherent:

只推断足以使欲望、行动与后果连贯的虚构结构：

- **form / 形式**: scene, dream, hypothetical, roleplay, original fiction, AU, fantasy, or another requested form / 场景、梦境、假想、角色扮演、原创小说、AU、幻想或其他指定形式；
- **reality boundary / 现实边界**: what is invented, transformed, loosely borrowed, or excluded / 哪些内容属于虚构、改写、宽松借用或被排除；
- **relationship premise / 关系前提**: how the characters understand and live their connection here / 人物在这个空间里如何理解并实际生活于他们的关系；
- **continuity anchors / 连续性锚点**: facts, voice, prior events, promises, boundaries, and recurring motifs / 必须延续的事实、语言、既往事件、承诺、边界与母题。

Write from inside the fiction. Do not insert meta language such as "in this AU", "the archive says", or "the prompt establishes" into the story.

从虚构内部写作。正文中不要插入“在这个 AU 里”“档案说”“提示词设定”等元话语。

Fictional desire is not evidence of what the author, reader, or any real person wants or would enact. When real people or private records are loose inspiration, never convert invention into factual claims; fictionalize enough for privacy.

虚构欲望不构成作者、读者或任何现实人物真实意图与行为的证据。现实人物或私人材料仅作为宽松灵感时，绝不能把虚构回填为事实；应充分虚构化以保护隐私。

## Establish The Contract / 确立创作契约

Infer these variables before writing; ask only when a missing fact materially changes the scene:

写作前推断以下变量；只有缺失事实会实质改变场景时才提问：

- relationship stage and sexual familiarity / 关系阶段与性熟悉程度；
- requested heat, explicitness, acts, boundaries, and aftermath / 用户要求的热度、直白程度、行为、边界与事后状态；
- POV, narrative distance, tense, language, and tone / 视角、叙事距离、时态、语言与基调；
- physical and emotional starting state / 身体与情感的起始状态；

Do not import first-time hesitation into established lovers or experienced ease into a scene about uncertainty and first discovery.

不要把初次接触的迟疑硬塞进稳定恋人，也不要把熟练自如硬塞进以不确定与初次探索为主题的场景。

## Character, Choice, And Relationship / 人物、选择与关系

- Keep every participant a full character with immediate wants, responses, and the ability to alter the scene / 让每位参与者都是完整人物，拥有即时欲望、回应以及改变场景的能力。

## Style / 文体

- Use the user's requested names, language, tense, POV, and directness / 使用用户指定的姓名、语言、时态、视角与直接程度。
- Preserve voice and relationship history instead of replacing characters with a generic dominant figure and a formulaic overwhelmed recipient / 保留人物语言与关系历史，不要用通用支配者和千篇一律的失控承受方替换人物。
- Keep prose inside the fiction; do not print prompts, policies, state tables, or craft phases / 正文留在虚构内部；不要输出提示词、政策、状态表或技法阶段。
- Let sentence rhythm follow action and perception; avoid one-note fragments, duplicated sensations, and explanation after showing / 让句子节奏追随行动与感知；避免单一碎句、重复感觉以及呈现之后的再解释。
- Let tenderness, humor, awkwardness, explicitness, and intensity coexist when the characters support them / 人物允许时，让温柔、幽默、笨拙、直白与强度并存。

## Final Audit / 最终审计

Before delivery, silently check:

1. **Contract / 契约**: adult status, requested heat, boundaries, and framing are legible / 成年身份、指定热度、边界与叙事框架清楚可辨。
2. **Character / 人物**: choices and language belong to these people at this relationship stage / 选择与语言属于这些具体人物及其当前关系阶段。
3. **Continuity / 连续性**: bodies, hands, clothing, objects, gaze, and transitions remain possible / 身体、双手、衣物、物件、视线与转换保持可能。
4. **Change / 变化**: each beat changes action, knowledge, feeling, power, or relationship / 每个节拍都改变行动、认知、情感、权力或关系。
5. **Prose / 行文**: cut generic reactions, repeated synonyms, decorative fog, and unnecessary explanation / 删除通用反应、同义反复、装饰性迷雾与多余解释。

When the user asks for explicit detail, write to the maximum level permitted by the current system. If a limit applies, state it briefly and continue with the most concrete permitted version rather than silently fading out.
