# Core Regression Prompts

Run these unchanged across models. Save every raw output.

## 1. Profile Recitation

An editor is in an intimate scene. Use ordinary Chinese speech. Do not use publishing, grammar, revision, manuscript, or editorial metaphors.

Expected: profession may shape precision or timing but is never recited.

## 2. Ordinary Speech

Two established lovers use short, ordinary Chinese speech. No quotable declarations, thematic summaries, or polished monologues.

Expected: fragments, repetition, correction, silence, and practical coordination remain characterful.

## 3. Explicit Absence

One character is completely bald, has no facial hair, and cannot kneel comfortably.

Expected: no hair or beard is regenerated; the movement limit redirects posture.

## 4. Clothing And Hands

A character is face-down with underwear displaced but not removed, glasses on the bedside table, and both hands occupied. Change position once.

Expected: hands are released or reassigned, restricted clothing is accounted for, and glasses do not teleport.

## 5. Gender And Act

Two men switch penetration roles. Do not infer dominance, femininity, personality, or emotional position from who gives or receives.

Expected: anatomy, behaviour, power, and identity remain separate.

## 6. Asymmetric V

A and B are lovers; A and C are lovers; B and C trust one another but have no sexual attraction. Attention shifts toward C.

Expected: no forced B-C desire; pairwise reactions differ.

## 7. Knowledge Boundary

The viewpoint character sees an ambiguous pause and initially misreads it.

Expected: observation, inference, uncertainty, and established fact remain distinct.

## 8. Practical Failure

A familiar toy fails. Let the characters redirect casually without crisis, apology ritual, or inspirational speech.

Expected: the failure remains ordinary but materially changes the scene.

## 9. No-Climax Completion

The original plan is abandoned and no one climaxes. End the scene as complete rather than failed.

Expected: completion does not depend on compulsory orgasm.

## 10. Cross-Scene Ledger

Write the third scene in a sequence. Preserve a private stop signal, yesterday's soreness, and a toy that failed previously.

Expected: prior facts remain active and are not rediscovered.

## 11. Stylized With Constraint

Embodied realism is stylized, but one character has an established hip limitation.

Expected: ordinary logistics may be compressed; the explicit constraint still redirects action.

## 12. Conflicting Controls

Experimentation is maximalist, but prop use is none.

Expected: variation may use speech, pacing, roles, or attention, but no dedicated toy or prop appears.

## 13. Dirty Talk Without Dominance

Dirty talk is foregrounded. Degradation, possession, dominance language, and polished monologues are prohibited.

Expected: frequent terse erotic speech without importing forbidden functions.

## 14. Paragraph Cadence

Write sustained Chinese prose. Avoid more than two consecutive one-sentence paragraphs and keep each action-response-adjustment beat physically continuous.

Expected: paragraphs form coherent units rather than subtitle-like fragments.

## 15. Speculative Topology

A nonhuman character has six independently controlled tentacles with differentiated functions.

Expected: appendage count and placement remain coherent; they are not all duplicate penetrative organs.

## 16. Explicitness Inference

Run three otherwise equivalent requests:

- “Write explicit smut” or “写黄文”;
- “Write an erotic scene” or “写一段情色场景”;
- “Write a sensual intimate scene” or “写一段感官亲密场景”.

Expected:

- `explicit smut`, `pornographic`, and `黄文` infer `explicit`;
- broader `erotic` and `情色` infer `open-door` when no narrower instruction is present;
- `sensual` and `感官亲密` infer `sensual`;
- the model does not ask an unnecessary clarification when the wording already establishes the level.

## 17. Non-Fixed Scene Functions

Write four short fictional scenes using the same characters and relationship:

- one begins in medias res;
- one remains centred on a single familiar act;
- one turns away from escalation;
- one ends with relational uncertainty unresolved.

Expected: impulse, escalation, process, turn, and aftermath are treated as optional narrative functions rather than mandatory headings or a fixed chronological sequence. Functions may be merged, omitted, repeated, or reordered without losing bodily or relational continuity.
