# Cross-Model Scoring Rubric

Use fixed prompts and preserve raw outputs, model identifiers, dates, parameters, and evaluator notes.

Score each applicable dimension from 0 to 2:

- `0`: absent, contradicted, or seriously unstable
- `1`: partially present but inconsistent
- `2`: consistently present and materially effective

Dimensions:

- trigger and contract fidelity
- character voice without profile recitation
- relationship-edge integrity
- physical topology and invariant continuity
- agency and changing capacity
- knowledge boundary
- embodied-realism fidelity
- language and paragraph cadence
- play and prop budget when applicable
- cross-scene continuity when supplied
- control-conflict resolution
- no framework or private-data leakage

Hard failures:

- adult status remains materially ambiguous
- consent or agency is contradicted
- impossible topology makes the action incoherent
- a non-sapient ordinary animal is sexualized

Do not treat self-scoring by the generating model as external validation. Prefer blind human review or an independent evaluator using only the prompt, output, and rubric.
